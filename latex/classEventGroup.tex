\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A grouping/area of the conference (e.\+g. a wing or a zone) that contains other Event\+Components. 

GoF roles\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Composite pattern\+: \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} is the Composite. It owns its children through explicitly-\/owned raw pointers, and recursively implements \doxylink{classEventGroup_aa12055a0456c9e6faf6de3a12a70b0e1}{open()}/close()/report\+Status()/get\+Capacity() by delegating to them.
\item \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} pattern, \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} role\+: an \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} can be attached to a parent \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject} (either another \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} or the root \doxylink{classEventControl}{Event\+Control}) so it hears about notices from above.
\item \doxylink{classObserver}{Observer} pattern, \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject} role\+: an \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} is also a \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject} to its own children -- when it hears a notice, it both reacts itself and cascades the notice to every child registered as its observer.
\end{DoxyItemize}

This dual Observer/\+Subject role is exactly what lets a notice originating at \doxylink{classEventControl}{Event\+Control} cascade through multiple composite levels \doxylink{classEventControl}{Event\+Control} -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} root \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} child \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} -\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} leaf \doxylink{classEventUnit}{Event\+Unit}.

Ownership policy\+: adding a child to an \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} transfers ownership to that \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} {\itshape and} automatically registers the child as an observer of this group. Event\+Flow does not use smart pointers (see project constraints); instead \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} owns its children through plain \textquotesingle{}Event\+Component\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}\textquotesingle{} stored in a \textquotesingle{}std\+::vector\textquotesingle{}, and \textquotesingle{}\doxylink{classEventGroup_a31ced71b330f350837e4dda2f461b94d}{\texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{\string~}\+Event\+Group()}\textquotesingle{} explicitly \textquotesingle{}delete\textquotesingle{}s each one exactly once. The two relationships -- ownership and registration -- are coupled deliberately here\+: anything physically inside a group should, by default, hear about notices that affect the group. (Task 11\+: "{}does every Composite observe its parent, does every child get auto-\/registered"{} -- see design rationale for the justification of this coupling, and for why it is {\itshape not} the only possible design.)

Self-\/reaction hook, analogous to \doxylink{classEventUnit_a896e33682a4c96fbcc970e807f5846bd}{Event\+Unit\+::react\+To\+Notice} but for group-\/level state (kept separate from cascading in \doxylink{classSubject_ab5b0a79cd1214e08f11dbe18611e8f8c}{notify()}). 

Per the current notice-\/reaction policy (see README.\+md), \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group} only reacts to\+: OPEN/\+CLOSE/\+EVACUATE (open/closed state -- required to affect {\itshape every} \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component}), CAPACITY\+\_\+\+ALERT (compares its own current\+Attendance\+\_\+ against capacity\+Threshold\+\_\+ via the shared \doxylink{classEventComponent_a9825b3edb52b95f7e7aa4264a025f1a8}{Event\+Component\+::report\+Capacity\+Alert()} helper -- also required to affect every \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component}), and ANNOUNCEMENT (a PA-\/style notice that, per policy, only ever affects Event\+Groups). PAUSE, RESUME, SCHEDULE\+\_\+\+CHANGE and CANCEL are deliberately {\itshape not} handled here -- those four only affect specific Concrete\+Leaves, never a group.
