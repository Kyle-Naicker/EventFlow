\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The event-\/level coordinator that originates notices. 

\doxylink{classEventControl}{Event\+Control} is deliberately not part of the Composite tree (Explained in Task 11\+: "{}\+Is Event\+Control itself part of the Composite or only a Subject/client collaborator"{}). Represents the human control-\/room staff issuing istructions. It is a concrete \doxylink{classSubject}{Subject}\+: whatever top-\/level Event\+Group/s are attached to it will receive and cascade its notices down through the whole tree.
