\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Common interface for anything that can appear in the Tech\+Connect conference\textquotesingle{}s part-\/whole tree\+: individual operational units (Leaves, see \doxylink{classEventUnit}{Event\+Unit}) and groupings of them (Composites, see \doxylink{classEventGroup}{Event\+Group}). 

A client holding an Event\+Component\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} can \doxylink{classEventComponent_ada5f41377769e0cbdaa7546477b3970c}{open()}, \doxylink{classEventComponent_a8e13a68d681c1b6affcf13435a09adab}{close()}, \doxylink{classEventComponent_ab71e0ee72d62a75756c926c78493cc94}{report\+Status()} and \doxylink{classEventComponent_a6128b4c0f20d353a29d45348f48a5f2e}{get\+Capacity()} without knowing or caring whether it is talking to a single room or an entire wing of the conference centre. This is the GoF Composite pattern\textquotesingle{}s Component role.

Every \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component} -- both Leaves and Composites -- carries a schedule (start\+Time\+\_\+/end\+Time\+\_\+) and a capacity-\/tracking pair (current\+Attendance\+\_\+/capacity\+Threshold\+\_\+), because OPEN, CLOSE, CAPACITY\+\_\+\+ALERT and EVACUATE are required to affect {\itshape every} kind of \doxylink{classEventComponent}{Event\+Component} uniformly (see README.\+md\textquotesingle{}s per-\/notice reaction table). Only \doxylink{classEventUnit}{Event\+Unit} (leaves) additionally track indoors\+\_\+/is\+Paused\+\_\+, since "{}indoors"{} and "{}paused"{} are only ever meaningful for an individual operational unit, not for a whole area.
