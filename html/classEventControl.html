Detailed Description

The event-level coordinator that originates notices.

EventControl is deliberately not part of the Composite tree (Explained in Task 11: "Is EventControl itself part of the Composite or only a Subject/client collaborator"). Represents the human control-room staff issuing istructions. It is a concrete Subject: whatever top-level EventGroup/s are attached to it will receive and cascade its notices down through the whole tree.
